# 00.2 — Decision Record — P1 Dual-Queue Core Loop

> **Decision:** Adopt the P1 structural direction as the new design baseline. This is a design decision, not a validated player outcome.
**Decision date:** 2026-10-01
## Problem
P0 proved that the Phaser rule sandbox could execute the loop reliably, but designer review exposed structural risks:
- Beast preparation can become speed/luck dominant with weak role expression.
- Resolution can arrive before the player forms a meaningful army plan.
- Matching Energy during active Battle makes Battle depend on puzzle input instead of progressing on its own.
- READY → CAST is close to mandatory execution rather than a timing decision.
## Adopted Direction
The current design baseline becomes:
**Deck Setup → Beast Rush → Energy Rush → Battle Setup / Beast Arrangement → Auto Battle + Timed Energy Cast → Result / Wave Transition → Next Cycle**
### Beast Rush
- Beast-only Onet board.
- Fast scanning and board luck are allowed to remain part of the puzzle identity.
- Successful Beast matches accumulate a **Beast Queue**.
- Beast role identity must be readable before Battle so the resulting army is understandable.
### Energy Rush
- Energy-only Onet board occurs **before** Battle.
- Successful Energy matches accumulate an **Energy Queue** for later use.
- Energy is no longer generated by matching during active Battle.
### Battle Setup
- Beast Queue is converted to combat units using the adopted STAR rule unless a later decision changes it.
- Player arranges Beast units on battlefield slots before active combat.
- Beast role should matter spatially and mechanically.
### Active Battle
- Combat progresses autonomously even when the player gives no input.
- Player focus is:
	1. the arrangement chosen before Battle,
	2. observing the combat state,
	3. casting queued Energy at useful moments.
- If the player never casts Energy, Battle still progresses and may end in victory or defeat.
- Energy timing must have a reason to matter; exact skill set and timing model remain Needs Validation.
## Preserved Rules
- Onet matching with ≤2 turns.
- 6×6 remains the current prototype board baseline unless changed separately.
- Pair generation and deadlock recovery remain required.
- Beast Queue remains content-specific.
- STAR-001 remains 1 / 3 / 9 for the current baseline.
## Retired P0 Structural Assumptions
These remain implementation history but are no longer the intended design baseline:
- Energy matching during active Auto Battle.
- Battle progression depending on repeated Energy matches.
- P0 Energy gauge +10 / max20 as the primary Battle interaction.
- Resolution immediately after Beast Rush as the final pre-Battle step.
## Open Decisions for P1
- Exact end condition / timing for Energy Rush.
- Exact Energy Queue conversion per match.
- Whether units can be repositioned after Battle starts.
- First Energy skill set and timing windows.
- Battlefield slot topology.
- How strongly Tanker / Assassin / Ranger / Mage roles differ in the first validation build.
- Whether Beast Rush keeps the exact P0 Combo values 5.0 / +0.3 / 5.0 or uses an Experimental variant.
## Validation Principle
This decision changes the **intended structure**, not its validation status.
The P1 implementation must test whether:
- fast/lucky puzzle collection can still feed meaningful Battle decisions,
- Beast roles become legible through arrangement,
- pre-collected Energy creates useful timing decisions,
- autonomous Battle creates a clear consequence for inaction.
Any tuning changes remain **Needs Validation** until real player evidence exists.
