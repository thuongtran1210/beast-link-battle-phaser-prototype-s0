# 03.5.1 — Experimental Variant P1-V2 — Beast Rush Timing

> **Status:** Proposed Experimental Variant — not adopted into Current Gameplay Spec.
**Finding:** <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3ec2674d32c3816c9958d5e133f90645">03.5 — Finding F-001 — Beast Rush Window Too Short</mention-page>
**P1-V1:** <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3ec2674d32c3816bb4f5eedcbc1b64b9"/>
## Objective
Test whether a longer Beast Rush window gives the player enough time to form matching / Beast-selection intent without changing the rest of the P1-V1 pre-Battle flow.
## Experimental Change
Change Beast Rush Combo timing only:
- Initial: **8.0s**
- Bonus per valid match: **+0.3s** (unchanged)
- Cap: **8.0s**
This changes only the Beast Rush window length. It does not increase the per-match bonus.
## Keep Unchanged
- 1.0s Energy Rush transition cue.
- EnergyRush countdown = **8.0s**.
- Energy match → +1 charge Experimental Variant A.
- BeastQueue / EnergyQueue behavior.
- STAR-001 1 / 3 / 9.
- Role mapping / formation.
- Autonomous Battle.
- Frontline Heal.
- P1 instrumentation.
## Why 8.0s
8.0s is a minimal, readable step from the current 5.0s window and matches the existing EnergyRush test window, while keeping the +0.3s recovery behavior unchanged.
This is a test value, not a balance conclusion.
## P03 Validation Focus
- Does Beast Rush still feel rushed?
- Can the tester form any deliberate Beast-selection intent before timeout?
- Does the longer window improve VQ-01 without making Beast Rush feel slow?
- Does the unchanged 8.0s EnergyRush still feel too short?
## Decision Discipline
- Do not retune EnergyRush in P1-V2.
- If EnergyRush 8.0s is again reported as too short in P03, that becomes repeated comparable evidence and can justify a separate Energy timing finding / variant.
- Do not update Current Gameplay Spec unless a later decision explicitly adopts the timing.
