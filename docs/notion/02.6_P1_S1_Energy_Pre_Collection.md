## Objective
Replace the P1-S0 EnergyRush navigation-only shell with real **pre-Battle Energy collection**.
Target: EnergyRush match → EnergyQueue → BattleSetup preview → Battle retains same stored charges.
Active Battle must remain free of puzzle matching.
## Experimental Fixture
The Current Gameplay Spec intentionally leaves exact Energy match → charge conversion open.
For **P1-S1 only**, use **ENERGY-001-VARIANT-A: 1 successful Energy pair match → +1 stored charge for that Energy ID**.
Status: **Experimental / prototype-only fixture**. This is not an adopted gameplay rule.
Do not reuse P0 +10 / max20 as the P1 rule.
## EnergyQueue
Create a small pure model/system with content-specific storage:
- energyId
- charges
Required operations:
- addCharge(energyId, amount = 1)
- getCharges(energyId)
- getAll()
- getTotalCharges()
- reset()
Requirements:
- separate Energy IDs accumulate independently,
- counts cannot become negative,
- read APIs do not expose mutable internal state,
- reset clears all stored charges.
Do not implement charge consumption yet. That is P1-S4 work.
## EnergyRush
- Keep fresh Energy-only 6×6 board.
- Reuse Onet / deadlock behavior.
- Each valid Energy match adds +1 charge to EnergyQueue for the matched Energy ID.
- Update visible Energy Queue summary.
- Do not touch BattleQueue.
- Do not mutate the historical P0 EnergySystem gauge.
- Keep the prototype-only **Continue to Battle Setup** action because EnergyRush termination remains an Open Decision.
## BattleSetup
- No puzzle board/input.
- Preserve Beast output summary.
- Add readable **Stored Energy** by Energy ID + total charges.
- Start Battle must not consume or reset EnergyQueue.
- No formation work yet.
## Battle
- No puzzle board/input.
- Display Stored Energy read-only so persistence can be verified.
- Do not add Cast action yet.
- Do not consume charges.
- Keep prototype-only **Complete S0 Battle** fixture until later slices replace it.
## Result / Restart
- Restart must reset EnergyQueue to empty.
- New BeastRush run must not inherit Energy charges.
## P0 Compatibility
Historical systems may stay in code but must not be wired into P1-S1:
- EnergySystem gauge
- +10 / max20 Ready rules
- active-Battle Energy matching
- P0 Cast Skill flow
## Deterministic Checks
1. new EnergyQueue starts empty,
2. one valid Energy match adds exactly +1 to matching Energy ID,
3. repeated same-ID matches accumulate,
4. different Energy IDs remain independent,
5. BeastQueue is unchanged by Energy matches,
6. EnergyQueue survives EnergyRush → BattleSetup,
7. EnergyQueue survives BattleSetup → Battle,
8. Start Battle does not consume charges,
9. Battle has no puzzle board/input,
10. no P0 EnergySystem gauge mutation occurs from P1 EnergyRush matches,
11. Restart clears EnergyQueue,
12. next BeastRush starts with zero Energy charges,
13. relevant P0 Onet / deadlock / STAR regressions pass,
14. P1-S0 transition checks remain green.
## Visible UI Check
EnergyRush should show that Energy matches are stored for Battle, with counts by Energy ID.
BattleSetup should show Beast output + Stored Energy.
Battle should retain the same Stored Energy counts but have no puzzle board.
