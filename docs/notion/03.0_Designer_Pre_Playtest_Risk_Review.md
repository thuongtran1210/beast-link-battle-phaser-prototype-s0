# 03.0 — Designer Pre-Playtest Risk Review

> **Source type:** Designer Review / Pre-validation rationale. These concerns contributed to the adopted P1 structural redesign in <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3ec2674d32c381e3bdb8fe9d409516f0"/>. They are still **not player evidence** and should not be presented as validated findings.

**Validation Log:** <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3ec2674d32c381da8c14df447e09cb92">03 — Validation Log — Beast Link Battle</mention-page>
**P0 Audit:** <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3ec2674d32c3814ab38ac952829289fa"/>
## Designer Assessment — Core Loop Risks
### DR-01 — Preparation may be speed/luck dominant instead of role-driven
Current Preparation asks the player to scan and match Beast tiles under a short Combo window. Beast role identity is not meaningfully represented in the current Phaser P0 presentation.
Risk:
- the dominant behavior may become "find any valid pair quickly",
- board availability/randomness may matter more than tactical intent,
- Tanker / Assassin / Ranger / Mage-style role differences are not visible enough to influence matching decisions,
- the phase may test Onet speed more than army planning.
This is a **design hypothesis**, not player evidence.
### DR-02 — Resolution may arrive before the player forms an army plan
Preparation can transition into Resolution quickly after the Combo window expires.
Risk:
- the player may not have enough time/context to understand what army they are building,
- Deploy / Start Battle may become a mechanical continuation button rather than a strategic decision,
- STAR 1 / 3 / 9 may create arithmetic choice without role/composition choice.
### DR-03 — Battle currently lacks consequence for player inaction
The P0 Battle model is intentionally simplified. Enemy Pressure changes through the prototype Damage skill, but the current sandbox does not represent a meaningful autonomous enemy threat over time.
Key questions:
- What happens if the player does not match Energy?
- What happens if the player does not cast Skill?
- Does Battle continue, deteriorate, or wait indefinitely?
- Is there an opportunity cost for ignoring the Energy puzzle?
Risk:
If "do nothing" has no consequence, Energy matching is not a tactical intervention; it is only the required input for advancing the prototype.
### DR-04 — Cast Skill may be mandatory execution rather than a choice
With one Damage skill and a pressure target that is primarily reduced by casting, READY → CAST can become an obvious mandatory button.
Risk:
- there is no reason to delay a cast,
- there is no competing skill/resource,
- timing may not matter,
- the player may not be making a decision, only completing a required step.
### DR-05 — Puzzle and Battle may still feel sequential rather than causally integrated
The current P0 proves the sequence:
Preparation → Resolution → Battle/Energy → Result.
It does not yet prove a strong two-way decision relationship between puzzle and battle.
Risk:
- Preparation affects counts/star output, but role/composition intent is weak,
- Battle gives little tactical information back to puzzle decisions,
- Energy phase acts as a separate charge-and-cast task rather than reactive battle support.
## Core Design Question
The next design iteration should answer:
**What meaningful decision does the player make in each phase that changes the next phase?**
A stronger loop should make these links explicit:
Preparation decision
→ Army composition / role coverage
→ Battle situation
→ Energy / Skill response
→ Battle outcome
→ Next preparation need
rather than only:
Match quickly
→ Convert counts
→ Match Energy
→ Cast until pressure reaches zero.
## Suggested P1 Design Goals
These are proposals for exploration, not adopted rules.
### Goal A — Make Preparation about composition, not only speed
Possible test directions:
- show Beast role clearly on tile / queue,
- preview upcoming enemy pressure or enemy role mix,
- make role selection matter to Battle,
- preserve Onet scanning but give the player a reason to prefer one valid pair over another.
### Goal B — Give Resolution a real decision
Possible test directions:
- show expected role contribution before Deploy,
- make Deploy-now vs save-for-star interact with army need,
- show enough Battle preview to support a decision.
### Goal C — Make Battle progress without player input
A tactical battle layer should have a baseline consequence if the player does nothing.
Possible prototype models:
- Enemy Pressure increases over time,
- Player Army HP/Power decreases over time,
- a timer advances toward defeat,
- enemy attacks create explicit damage events.
Energy matching then becomes intervention, not the sole engine that moves the battle.
### Goal D — Give casting timing a reason
A skill should have at least one timing consideration.
Examples for later variants:
- Damage is more valuable before an enemy threshold,
- Heal is wasted at full HP,
- Slow is useful before an enemy attack window,
- save Energy for a dangerous moment vs cast immediately.
Do not add multiple systems at once. First prove that "do nothing" has a visible consequence.
## Validation Impact
Before changing the Current Gameplay Spec:
1. Run P01 as a **diagnostic baseline session**.
2. Specifically observe whether the tester:
	- matches any available Beast without role preference,
	- understands what kind of army is being built,
	- sees a reason to use Individual Deploy,
	- expects Battle to progress without Energy input,
	- sees any reason not to cast immediately at READY.
3. Treat these observations as evidence only after the session is recorded.
4. If the risks are confirmed, create a Design Proposal and an Experimental P1 variant.
## Current Outcome
The project owner adopted the P1 structural direction before the main player-test round. P0 remains a verified implementation baseline, but the main validation round is postponed until the P1 structural prototype is synchronized.
