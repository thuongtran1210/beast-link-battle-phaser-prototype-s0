# 03.10 — Experimental Variant P1-V5 — Enemy Squad Battle Grid

> **Status:** Experimental · implemented in Phaser code · verification pending. Triggered by P03 owner feedback that the single-enemy battle is too weak and does not match the intended opposing-grid battle presentation. Not adopted into Current Gameplay Spec.
## Reference Direction
Target composition follows the owner-provided battle reference: player formation occupies one side of the battle field and enemy units occupy a mirrored opposing grid. The Phaser prototype may use simplified shapes/hex slots; final art is out of scope.
## Experimental Enemy Fixture
- Enemy squad size: **4**
- Each enemy: **65 HP / 6 damage**
- Aggregate opening pressure: **260 HP / 24 damage per tick**
- Enemy slots:
	- Enemy A — Front / column 2
	- Enemy B — Front / column 5
	- Enemy C — Mid / column 3
	- Enemy D — Mid / column 4
## Combat Rules for This Variant
- Existing player formation remains 3×6 Front / Mid / Back.
- Enemy side mirrors the same 3×6 battle-field structure for presentation.
- Player total living-unit damage is applied to enemy targets in Front → Mid → Back, then lower column priority, spilling to the next enemy if a target is defeated during the same tick.
- All living enemy damage is summed and applied to the current player frontline target.
- As enemies die, incoming damage decreases.
- Existing Energy Frontline Heal, player role stats, STAR multipliers, 1.0s battle tick, Result flow, and formation lock remain unchanged.
## Backward Compatibility
- Default AutonomousBattleModel fixture remains the historical single enemy (150 HP / 15 damage) for P1-S3/P1-S4 regression checks.
- P1-V5 enemy squad is injected only by the validation scene / explicit experimental tests.
## Validation Goal
Test whether a mirrored multi-unit enemy field creates enough combat pressure and visual clarity to make formation and timed Energy decisions meaningful.
## Non-Goals
- No final enemy roster design.
- No enemy skills/roles.
- No wave system.
- No boss mechanics.
- No production balance claim.
## Implementation Update — 2026-10-02
- `AutonomousBattleModel` now accepts injected enemy fixtures while preserving the historical single-enemy default for P1-S3/P1-S4 regression.
- P1-V5 validation scene injects 4 enemies at 65 HP / 6 damage each.
- Player damage targets enemy Front → Mid → Back with column priority and can spill to the next target in the same tick.
- Living enemy damage aggregates against the current player frontline; pressure decreases as enemies die.
- `BattleActionView` now renders mirrored player/enemy formation grids within the 960×760 validation canvas.
- Added `P1V5Checks.ts` and regression-runner integration.
- Result → Restart hotfix remains part of the verification gate.
