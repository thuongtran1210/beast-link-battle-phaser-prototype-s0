# 03.12 — Experimental Variant P1-V7 — Combat Pressure Tuning

> **Status:** Experimental · implemented · check/build/live gate passed by project-owner confirmation on 2026-10-02. Ready for P03 retest. Not adopted into Current Gameplay Spec.
## Problem
The current P1-V5 enemy squad is visually clearer but dies too quickly. A 1–2 second Battle is too short to evaluate:
- formation pressure,
- frontline change,
- enemy attrition,
- Energy Heal timing,
- cast-now vs save-for-later decisions.
## Validation Target
Prototype Battle should usually last roughly **10–20 seconds** for a representative all-1★ formation. This is a validation target, not a production balance target.
## Experimental Fixture
- Enemy squad size: **6**
- Each enemy: **160 HP / 3 damage**
- Aggregate opening HP: **960**
- Aggregate opening pressure: **18 damage / tick**
- Enemy positions:
	- A — Front / lane 1
	- B — Front / lane 3
	- C — Front / lane 5
	- D — Mid / lane 2
	- E — Mid / lane 4
	- F — Mid / lane 6
## Preserved Rules
- 1.0 second Battle tick.
- Existing player role stats / STAR multipliers.
- Existing target priority and damage-spill behavior.
- Incoming enemy pressure falls as enemies die.
- Frontline Heal remains +30 HP capped, finite stored Energy.
- P1-V6 integrated Setup/Battle battlefield remains unchanged.
## Deterministic Validation Check
A representative 7-unit all-1★ formation is used as a duration fixture.
Expected gate:
- must not resolve before 10 ticks,
- should resolve within 30 ticks,
- enemy pressure must visibly decrease as enemies die.
## Non-Goals
- No final difficulty curve.
- No final enemy roster.
- No enemy skills.
- No waves/bosses.
- No production balance claim.
