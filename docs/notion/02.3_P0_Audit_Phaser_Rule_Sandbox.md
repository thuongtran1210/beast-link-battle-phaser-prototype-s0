# 02.3 — P0 Audit — Phaser Rule Sandbox

> **Post-audit design update:** P0 remains implementation-complete historical evidence. Current Gameplay Spec v2 now adopts a different P1 structure. Use this audit to understand what P0 proved technically, not as the current design target. P1 decision: <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3ec2674d32c381e3bdb8fe9d409516f0"/>.
> **Purpose:** record what P0 currently proves at the implementation level, what remains unvalidated by players, and what evidence the first real playtest must collect. This page does not redefine gameplay rules.

## Audit Conclusion
**P0 Rule Sandbox is implementation-complete for its intended validation scope.**
Evidence currently available:
- S1–S5 deterministic checks pass.
- Production build passes.
- Live browser flow is verified through **Preparation → Resolution → Battle → Result → Session Summary → Restart → fresh Preparation**.
- Battle board exhaustion is verified to continue with a fresh Energy-only board instead of dead-ending.
- Result presentation and Restart flow are readable enough for validation use.
This proves the prototype can execute the intended P0 validation loop. It does **not** prove that the current tuning, UX, or design decisions are good for players.

## What P0 Does Not Prove
- That 6×6 is the best board size.
- That Combo 5.0s / +0.3s / 5.0 cap creates the right urgency.
- That Resolution is understood without explanation.
- That 1 / 3 / 9 produces good army growth or meaningful save-vs-deploy decisions.
- That +10 / max 20 creates the right Energy skill frequency.
- That phase separation is better than a mixed Beast + Energy board.
- That the simplified pressure battle represents final combat balance.
- Retention, engagement, monetization, progression or production difficulty claims.
## P0 Validation Targets
These are the first questions the playtest must answer:
1. **Match → Queue comprehension:** does the player understand that Beast matching creates Battle Queue value rather than instantly spawning a unit?
2. **Combo pacing:** does 5.0 / +0.3 / 5.0 create useful urgency without making Onet scanning feel unfair or unreadable?
3. **Resolution comprehension:** does the player understand why the game stops puzzle input and what Deploy / Start Battle mean?
4. **STAR-001 agency:** does 1 / 3 / 9 create an understandable choice between deploying now and saving for a higher star?
5. **Energy loop comprehension:** does the player understand Energy Match → Gauge → Ready → Cast and recognize its effect on Battle?
6. **Phase readability:** can the player distinguish Preparation, Resolution, Battle and Result without moderator explanation?

## Known Prototype-only Conditions
- Enemy Pressure = 90.
- Damage Skill = -10 pressure.
- Battle board exhaustion can generate a fresh Energy-only board to keep the validation run playable.
- Placeholder letters represent Beast / Energy content.
- No production combat AI, wave system, meta progression or production art is included.
