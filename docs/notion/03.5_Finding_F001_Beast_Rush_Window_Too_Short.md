# 03.5 — Finding F-001 — Beast Rush Window Too Short

> **Status:** Confirmed repeated qualitative finding. Does not change Current Gameplay Spec.
**Evidence:** <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3ec2674d32c381048288f33c8c133cd8"/> · <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3ec2674d32c381899d10efdc4ffaf214"/>
## Finding
Across P01 and P02, Beast Rush is consistently experienced as too short to support deliberate matching / Beast-selection intent before the phase ends.
## Evidence
- P01: Beast Rush timing felt too short; the phase moved on before there was much room to read the board or form intent.
- P02: session feedback again reported the Beast Rush countdown as too short while Beast tuning remained unchanged.
## Scope
This finding applies to Beast Rush timing only.
It does **not** confirm that the Experimental EnergyRush 8.0s timer is wrong. The 8.0s Energy window being too short currently has only P02-comparable evidence.
## Related Validation Question
**VQ-01** — Can fast/lucky Beast Rush output still produce an army the player can understand and arrange meaningfully?
## Severity / Impact
- Frequency: 2 / 2 sessions reporting Beast Rush pacing concern.
- Severity: Medium.
- Impact on Core Loop: High, because the collection phase may end before the player can form intentional Beast-selection behavior.
## Next Step
Test the smallest isolated timing change: increase the Beast Rush window while keeping EnergyRush P1-V1 unchanged.
