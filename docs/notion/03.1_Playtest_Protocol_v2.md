# 03.1 — Playtest Protocol v2 — P1 Structural Loop

> **Status:** Active P1 playtest protocol. Use this for the main real-player validation round against the current P1 structural baseline.
**Validation Log:** <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3ec2674d32c381da8c14df447e09cb92">03 — Validation Log — Beast Link Battle</mention-page>
**P1 Exit Audit:** <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3ec2674d32c3810daf83fb66890a64ea"/>
**Design Source:** <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3ec2674d32c3812296a8de866c65c434"/>
## Test Purpose
Validate whether the P1 loop is understandable and creates meaningful decisions:
**Beast Rush → Energy Rush → Battle Setup / Beast Arrangement → Autonomous Battle + Timed Energy Cast → Result**
Primary questions:
- **VQ-01:** Can fast/lucky Beast Rush output still produce an army the player can understand and arrange meaningfully?
- **VQ-02:** Are Beast roles readable enough to influence placement rather than feeling interchangeable?
- **VQ-03:** Does Energy Rush communicate that Energy is being stored for later Battle use?
- **VQ-04:** Does autonomous Battle create a clear consequence when the player gives no input?
- **VQ-05:** Does finite pre-collected Energy create a meaningful cast-now vs save-for-later timing decision?
- **VQ-06:** Does STAR 1 / 3 / 9 interact with role/formation choice in a way the player can understand?
## What This Round Does Not Validate
Do not use this round to claim final balance, production combat depth, retention, monetization, progression, final art/UI, final Energy conversion, final role stats, final formation topology, or final skill set.
Current Energy conversion, role mapping/stats, formation grid, combat values, and Frontline Heal are **Experimental prototype fixtures**.
## Recommended Round
Run **5–8 individual sessions** before making a cross-player design conclusion, unless a severe blocker prevents meaningful testing.
Keep the P1 baseline unchanged during the round. If a severe blocker appears, log the session and blocker first, then create a separate proposal/variant.

## Moderator Opening
> “Bạn hãy chơi prototype này như một game mới. Trong lúc chơi, nếu có thể hãy nói ra bạn đang nghĩ game muốn bạn làm gì và vì sao bạn chọn hành động đó. Mình sẽ hạn chế hướng dẫn để xem gameplay tự truyền đạt được đến đâu.”
Do not explain in advance:
- Beast roles,
- STAR 1 / 3 / 9,
- why Front / Mid / Back matter,
- what Energy will be used for later,
- when the player should cast Heal,
- the enemy target-priority rule.
If the tester asks what to do, first reply:
> “Bạn thử làm theo những gì bạn hiểu từ màn hình nhé.”
Only intervene if the run cannot continue. Record every intervention.
## Observation Sequence
### 1. Beast Rush — Collection Behavior
Observe how quickly the tester understands Onet matching, whether they focus only on the first available pair, whether they notice Beast identity / queue accumulation, whether speed/luck feels dominant, and whether they form any expectation about what collected Beasts will become.
Do not teach role meaning here.
Automatic evidence:
- beastMatches
- beastQueueAtSetup
### 2. Energy Rush — Stored Resource Comprehension
Observe whether the tester recognizes the phase change, understands Energy matches are being stored, expects Energy to be used immediately or later, notices different Energy IDs / charge counts, and understands why Energy is collected before Battle.
Automatic evidence:
- energyMatches
- energyChargesAtBattleStart
Do not explain the Experimental 1 pair → +1 charge rule unless the tester cannot continue.
### 3. Battle Setup — Roles, STAR and Formation
Observe whether the tester reads Beast ID / Role / Star, whether Tanker / Assassin / Ranger / Mage labels create different placement expectations, whether they deliberately use Front / Mid / Back, whether they move an already-placed unit after reconsidering, whether higher-star units affect placement priority, and whether Start Battle readiness is understood.
Automatic evidence:
- roleCounts
- starTierCounts
- arrangementChanges
- timeInBattleSetup
### 4. Autonomous Battle — Consequence of Inaction
At Battle start, do not tell the tester to cast immediately.
Observe whether they recognize combat is progressing automatically, notice HP changing without input, connect formation position to who takes damage first, expect puzzle matching during Battle, and understand that doing nothing still has consequences.
Automatic evidence:
- battleDuration
- resultWinLose
### 5. Timed Energy Cast
Observe when the tester first considers casting, whether they watch CURRENT FRONTLINE HP, whether they cast at/near full HP, wait for damage, save charges, understand charges are finite, and notice exactly what changed after a cast.
Do not say that delayed healing is better. Record timing and stated reason.
Automatic evidence:
- firstCastTime
- castsUsed
- armyHpAtFirstCast
- enemyHpAtFirstCast
- unusedChargesAtResult
### 6. Result / Restart
Observe whether the tester recognizes Win/Lose, whether the P1 Session Summary is readable enough for the moderator to capture evidence, whether Restart is understood, and whether the fresh run appears clean.
The Session Summary is instrumentation; player interest in it is not itself a gameplay finding.
## Post-test Questions
1. **Ở Beast Rush, bạn nghĩ mình đang thu thập Beast để làm gì?**
2. **Khi sang Energy Rush, bạn nghĩ Energy sẽ được dùng khi nào và để làm gì?**
3. **Ở Battle Setup, điều gì khiến bạn đặt từng Beast vào Front, Mid hoặc Back?**
4. **Bạn hiểu các role Tanker / Assassin / Ranger / Mage khác nhau như thế nào từ prototype?**
5. **STAR của Beast có ảnh hưởng đến quyết định xếp đội hình của bạn không? Vì sao?**
6. **Khi Battle bắt đầu, bạn có nghĩ mình cần tiếp tục làm puzzle không?**
7. **Bạn nhận ra Battle vẫn tự diễn ra bằng dấu hiệu nào?**
8. **Bạn quyết định lúc nào dùng Energy/Heal dựa trên điều gì?**
9. **Có lúc nào bạn muốn giữ Energy lại thay vì dùng ngay không? Vì sao?**
10. **Phase hoặc thời điểm nào khiến bạn không biết mình nên làm gì tiếp theo?**
11. **Nếu chỉ thay đổi một thứ để loop này rõ hoặc thú vị hơn, bạn sẽ thay đổi gì?**
Neutral follow-ups:
- “Bạn nhận ra điều đó từ đâu?”
- “Lúc đó bạn đang nghĩ gì?”
- “Điều gì trên màn hình khiến bạn chọn như vậy?”
- “Bạn đã mong đợi điều gì xảy ra tiếp theo?”
Avoid leading questions such as:
- “Tanker nên đứng Front đúng không?”
- “Heal dùng lúc máu thấp sẽ tốt hơn đúng không?”
- “Energy Rush có rõ là để tích skill không?”
- “STAR 1/3/9 có cân bằng không?”
## Evidence Discipline
For every session, keep these separate:
- **Observed Behavior** — what the tester actually did.
- **Player Statement** — what the tester said.
- **Metric** — values recorded by the prototype.
- **Designer Interpretation** — your interpretation for this tester only.
Do not turn one session into a cross-player finding.
## Cross-player Review
After 5–8 sessions:
1. group evidence by VQ-01 → VQ-06,
2. separate comprehension problems from tuning/balance complaints,
3. look for repeated behavior, not isolated quotes,
4. prioritize by **Frequency × Severity × Impact on Core Loop**,
5. create Validation Findings only for supported patterns.
## Change Discipline
During the round, do not edit the Current Gameplay Spec because of a single session.
Use:
**Validation Finding → Design Proposal → Experimental Variant → Retest → Adopt / Reject**.
P1 remains the baseline comparison condition until a later decision is explicitly adopted.
