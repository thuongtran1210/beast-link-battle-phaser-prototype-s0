## Objective
Instrument the completed P1 structural loop without changing gameplay.
The Result / Session Summary must capture enough evidence to evaluate:
- fast Beast collection,
- Energy pre-collection,
- army composition / STAR output,
- formation reconsideration,
- autonomous Battle duration/outcome,
- Energy cast timing and remaining resources.
Do not tune gameplay values in this slice.
## P1 Metrics
Extend or replace the legacy P0 `SessionMetrics` presentation with these P1 metrics.
### Puzzle / Queue
- `beastMatches` — successful matches during BeastRush only.
- `energyMatches` — successful matches during EnergyRush only.
- `beastQueueAtSetup` — immutable snapshot of BeastQueue when entering BattleSetup; preserve total and per-Beast counts.
- `energyChargesAtBattleStart` — immutable snapshot of stored Energy immediately when Start Battle succeeds; preserve total and per-Energy counts.
### Army / Setup
- `roleCounts` — count converted units by Tanker / Assassin / Ranger / Mage at Battle start.
- `starTierCounts` — count 1★ / 2★ / 3★ units at Battle start.
- `arrangementChanges` — count successful moves of an already-placed unit to a different slot. Initial placement does **not** increment this metric.
- `timeInBattleSetup` — seconds from entering BattleSetup until successful Start Battle.
### Battle / Energy Timing
- `firstCastTime` — seconds from Battle start to first successful Energy cast; `—` / null if no cast.
- `castsUsed` — successful casts only.
- `unusedChargesAtResult` — total remaining stored Energy at terminal Result; preserve per-ID counts if convenient.
- `battleDuration` — seconds from Battle start to terminal Win/Lose.
- `resultWinLose` — Win or Lose.
- `armyHpAtFirstCast` — sum of current HP across all player units immediately before the first successful cast; null if no cast.
- `enemyHpAtFirstCast` — current Enemy HP immediately before the first successful cast; null if no cast.
Legacy P0 metrics may remain internally for regression compatibility, but the visible P1 Session Summary should not present obsolete concepts such as `First Skill Ready`, active-Battle Energy gauge, or P0 Resolution deploy counts as if they were current P1 validation metrics.
## Snapshot Rules
- Queue snapshots must not change after capture.
- Battle-start snapshots are captured only once.
- First-cast metrics are captured only on the first **successful** cast.
- Rejected casts must not increment `castsUsed` or populate first-cast metrics.
- Result metrics freeze when Battle reaches Win/Lose.
- Rendering the summary must not mutate metrics.
## Result / Session Summary UI
Keep the readable Result panel, but organize it around the P1 flow.
Recommended sections:
### COLLECTION
- Beast Matches
- Beast Queue at Setup — total
- Energy Matches
- Energy Charges at Battle Start — total
### ARMY / SETUP
- Role Counts
- Star Tiers
- Formation Repositions
- Time in Battle Setup
### BATTLE
- Outcome
- Battle Duration
- First Cast Time
- Casts Used
- Army HP at First Cast
- Enemy HP at First Cast
- Unused Energy at Result
Per-ID Beast / Energy snapshots may be shown in smaller debug text if needed, but keep the main summary readable.
## Restart
`Restart Validation Run` must clear all P1 metrics and begin a fresh BeastRush session.
No snapshot, timer, count, outcome, or first-cast state may leak into the next run.
## Deterministic Checks
Add P1-S5 checks for:
1. new session starts with all P1 counters/snapshots empty or zero,
2. BeastRush valid match increments `beastMatches` only,
3. EnergyRush valid match increments `energyMatches` only,
4. `beastQueueAtSetup` is captured once and remains immutable after conversion,
5. `energyChargesAtBattleStart` is captured once and remains immutable after casts,
6. roleCounts reflect converted units at Battle start,
7. starTierCounts reflect STAR-001 output at Battle start,
8. initial slot placements do not increment `arrangementChanges`,
9. moving an already-placed unit to a different slot increments `arrangementChanges` exactly once,
10. `timeInBattleSetup` records once when Start Battle succeeds,
11. rejected cast does not increment `castsUsed`,
12. first successful cast records `firstCastTime`, `armyHpAtFirstCast`, and `enemyHpAtFirstCast` once,
13. later successful casts increment `castsUsed` but do not overwrite first-cast snapshots,
14. `battleDuration` and `resultWinLose` freeze at terminal state,
15. `unusedChargesAtResult` matches remaining EnergyQueue at Result,
16. summary rendering does not mutate metrics,
17. Restart fully clears P1 metrics,
18. P1-S0/S1/S2/S3/S4 plus reusable Onet/deadlock/STAR regressions remain green.
## Live Verification
Run one full P1 session:
1. perform several Beast matches,
2. perform several Energy matches,
3. enter BattleSetup and move at least one already-placed unit to another slot,
4. Start Battle,
5. wait for damage,
6. cast at least one Energy charge,
7. allow Battle to reach Result automatically,
8. verify the P1 Session Summary values correspond to the run,
9. Restart and verify a fresh run starts with cleared metrics.
Also verify a no-cast run if practical:
- `firstCastTime` = —,
- `castsUsed` = 0,
- Battle still reaches Win/Lose,
- unused Energy remains correctly recorded.
