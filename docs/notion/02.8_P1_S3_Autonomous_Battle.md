## Objective
Replace the prototype-only manual Battle completion fixture with a minimal **autonomous combat model**.
Battle must answer the structural question:
**If the player gives zero input, does combat still progress toward a visible Win or Lose state?**
Energy remains stored and read-only in P1-S3. Energy casting is P1-S4.
## Combat Model
Create a pure deterministic model, e.g. `AutonomousBattleModel`, that can advance one fixed combat tick at a time.
Minimum state:
- player units from the locked BattleFormation,
- each unit current HP / max HP / damage / role / star / slot,
- enemy current HP / max HP,
- battle status: Running / Win / Lose,
- elapsed ticks.
## Experimental Combat Fixtures
These values are **prototype-only / Experimental**, not adopted balance rules.
### Role base stats
- Tanker: HP 80, Damage 6
- Assassin: HP 35, Damage 14
- Ranger: HP 45, Damage 10
- Mage: HP 40, Damage 9
### Star multiplier
Apply the same temporary multiplier to HP and Damage:
- 1★ = ×1.0
- 2★ = ×1.8
- 3★ = ×3.2
### Enemy fixture
- Enemy HP = 150
- Enemy Damage per tick = 15
### Tick timing
- One combat tick = 1.0 second in the visible prototype.
- Deterministic tests should call the pure tick function directly rather than depend on wall-clock timing.
## Autonomous Tick Rule
For each tick while status = Running:
1. Every alive player unit deals its Damage to the enemy.
2. If Enemy HP \<= 0, set status = Win and stop the tick.
3. Otherwise the enemy attacks exactly one player unit.
4. Enemy target priority = nearest occupied row to the enemy: Front → Mid → Back.
5. Within that row, target the lowest column / stable slot order.
6. Apply Enemy Damage.
7. If the target reaches 0 HP, it remains defeated and is skipped on later ticks.
8. If no player units remain alive while Enemy HP \> 0, set status = Lose.
This rule intentionally makes row placement matter without implementing movement, pathfinding, or target AI.
## Role / Position Meaning in P1-S3
- Tanker has the highest HP and is naturally suited to absorb front-row hits.
- Assassin has high damage but low HP, so exposing it early is risky.
- Ranger / Mage are safer when protected behind another occupied row.
Do not add hidden row bonuses or penalties. Position matters only through enemy target priority.
## Battle Start
When Start Battle is pressed:
- formation becomes locked/read-only,
- instantiate combat units from the exact placed formation,
- apply experimental role stats + star multiplier,
- initialize enemy fixture,
- begin autonomous ticks automatically.
No puzzle input. No formation edits. No Energy consumption.
## Battle UI
Show enough validation information to observe autonomous progress:
- Enemy HP current / max,
- each unit: Beast ID, Role, Star, row/slot, current HP / max HP,
- Stored Energy read-only,
- Battle Status: Running / Win / Lose.
Remove the `Complete S0 Battle` manual completion button from the live P1-S3 flow.
## Result
- Win automatically transitions to Result.
- Lose automatically transitions to Result.
- Result must clearly identify Win or Lose.
- Stored Energy is not consumed in P1-S3.
- Restart behavior remains unchanged and starts a clean BeastRush.
## Deterministic Checks
Add P1-S3 checks for:
1. Battle starts in Running state with Enemy HP 150.
2. Role base stats are deterministic.
3. Star multiplier is applied to HP and Damage.
4. All alive player units attack every tick.
5. Enemy targets Front before Mid before Back.
6. Within a row, targeting uses stable lowest-column/slot order.
7. Defeated units stop attacking and cannot be targeted again.
8. Battle advances without any player action.
9. A strong fixture can reach Win with zero player input.
10. A weak/exposed fixture can reach Lose with zero player input.
11. Win stops further HP changes.
12. Lose stops further HP changes.
13. Formation remains unchanged/read-only during Battle.
14. EnergyQueue remains unchanged throughout Battle.
15. No puzzle board/input exists during Battle.
16. Restart clears combat/formation/queue state.
17. P1-S0/S1/S2 and reusable Onet/deadlock/STAR checks remain green.
## Visible Live Verification
Use at least two runs if practical:
### Run A — Autonomous progression
- Arrange units.
- Start Battle.
- Give no input.
- Confirm HP values change every second.
- Confirm front-most occupied row receives enemy damage first.
- Confirm Battle reaches Win or Lose automatically.
- Confirm Result appears automatically.
### Run B — Formation consequence
- Place a fragile unit in front with a tougher unit behind, or otherwise change row order.
- Confirm the front unit receives damage first.
- This only proves positional consequence, not balance quality.
## Forbidden in P1-S3
Do not implement:
- Energy charge consumption,
- Cast buttons,
- Heal / Shield / Damage skills,
- manual targeting,
- movement/pathfinding,
- attack range simulation,
- attack-speed systems,
- row bonuses,
- mid-combat reposition,
- multiple enemies,
- waves,
- production balance tuning.
## Completion Rule
P1-S3 is complete only when deterministic checks + production build + live browser prove:
- Battle progresses with zero player input,
- formation position has visible consequence through target priority,
- both Win and Lose are possible in the model,
- Result is reached automatically,
- Stored Energy remains untouched.
