# 03.13 — Experimental Variant P1-V8 — Role Identity & Positional Combat

> **Status:** Experimental · implemented · check/build/live gate passed by project-owner confirmation on 2026-10-02. Ready for P03 fresh retest. Not adopted into Current Gameplay Spec.
## Confirmed Progress from P1-V7
- Battle duration is now long enough to create a meaningful Energy Heal timing window.
- Enemy pressure / attrition can be observed.
## New Problem
Current player damage is still effectively aggregated from every living unit each tick. This makes role/placement consequences too weak:
- Ranger/Mage do not clearly gain a tactical reason to occupy Mid/Back.
- Assassin does not visibly bypass the frontline.
- Lane choice has little offensive meaning.
- Units feel differentiated mainly by HP/damage/color rather than behavior.
## P1-V8 Experimental Role Behaviors
### Tanker — Guard Strike
- Full attack only from Front.
- Targets the nearest enemy frontline, preferring the closest lane.
- Placement intent: occupy Front to protect the army and still contribute damage.
### Assassin — Dive
- Active from Front or Mid.
- Prioritizes the deepest living enemy row (Back → Mid → Front), then nearest lane.
- Presentation must visibly leap/dive to the chosen target and return.
- Placement intent: Front/Mid creates access to vulnerable deeper targets.
### Ranger — Snipe
- Can attack from all rows.
- Damage by player row: Front 60%, Mid 80%, Back 100%.
- Prefers the deepest enemy in the same lane; otherwise nearest lane.
- Presentation fires a projectile to the actual selected target.
- Placement intent: Back gives best ranged output while remaining protected.
### Mage — Arcane Burst
- Can attack from all rows.
- Front 70%, Mid/Back 100%.
- Selects a deep/near-lane primary target and also damages adjacent-lane enemies at 50% of the primary damage.
- Presentation shows the primary impact plus secondary pulse/impacts.
- Placement intent: lane choice can create multi-target value.
## Preserved Rules
- P1-V7 enemy fixture and combat-duration target.
- 1.0 second battle tick.
- Existing role base HP/damage and STAR multipliers.
- Existing enemy aggregate damage → current player frontline.
- Existing finite Stored Energy and +30 Frontline Heal.
- Existing Result/Restart and integrated mirrored battlefield.
## Backward Compatibility
- Legacy aggregate player damage remains the default AutonomousBattleModel behavior for historical P1-S3/P1-S4/P1-V7 regression.
- P1-V8 role-position combat is opt-in only in the validation scene / V8 tests.
## Validation Goal
Determine whether players can explain:
1. why Tanker belongs at Front,
2. why Ranger benefits from Back,
3. what Assassin is trying to reach,
4. whether lane selection matters,
5. whether units feel behaviorally distinct rather than just stat blocks.
## Non-Goals
- No active skills.
- No mana/cooldowns/status effects.
- No final role balance.
- No enemy role AI.
- No Current Gameplay Spec adoption.
