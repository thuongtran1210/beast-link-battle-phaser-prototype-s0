# 03.9 — Experimental Variant P1-V4 — Battle Action Readability

> **Status:** Proposed Experimental presentation variant. Triggered by P03 designer observation. Not adopted into Current Gameplay Spec.
## Problem
The current autonomous Battle is mechanically functional but visually static. Player-facing consequences are mostly text, HP values, and buttons. This limits the validity of VQ-04/VQ-05 because the player cannot clearly read who attacked, who was hit, when a unit died, or how a heal changed the battle state.
## Scope
Presentation-only. Preserve the existing AutonomousBattleModel math, deterministic 1.0s tick cadence, targeting, damage values, Energy consumption, Frontline Heal amount, win/lose rules, and formation logic.
## Minimum Action Presentation
- Each living Beast visibly performs an attack action on its battle tick.
- Tanker / Assassin may use short melee lunge/contact feedback.
- Ranger / Mage may use a simple projectile/beam-style visual.
- Enemy visibly reacts to player damage.
- Current enemy attack target visibly receives a hit reaction.
- HP changes remain visible and synchronized with the existing model.
- Unit death has a clear visual state (fade/collapse/remove from active emphasis).
- Frontline Heal has a visible heal pulse/flash and HP recovery feedback.
- Energy charge consumption remains finite and unchanged.
- No new skill, cooldown, damage, speed, crit, status effect, or role rule.
## Validation Goal
Make autonomous Battle consequences readable enough that a real player can judge:
1. who is acting,
2. who is being targeted,
3. when damage occurs,
4. when a unit dies,
5. whether heal timing mattered.
## Non-Goals
- No final art.
- No production animation system.
- No balance changes.
- No new combat mechanics.
- No change to Current Gameplay Spec.
## Exit Gate
- Existing deterministic battle checks still pass.
- Production build passes.
- Full flow still works.
- Live browser shows readable attack / hit / death / heal feedback.
- P03 is restarted only after this presentation gate passes, because earlier P03 battle-feel evidence is not sufficient for VQ-04/VQ-05.
