## Objective
Implement the smallest Experimental pre-Battle clarity variant for P02:
**Beast Rush → 1.0s Energy Rush transition cue → Energy Rush with visible 8.0s countdown → automatic Battle Setup**
Do not change Beast Rush tuning in this variant.
## Change A — Energy Rush Transition Cue
When Beast Rush ends:
- stop Beast board interaction immediately,
- do not enter interactive EnergyRush yet,
- show a centered validation-only transition card for **1.0 second**:
	- `ENERGY RUSH`
	- `Collect Energy for Battle`
- after 1.0 second, enter interactive EnergyRush.
Implementation guidance:
- Prefer an overlay / transition substate owned by ValidationScene rather than adding a new canonical GamePhase.
- The 1.0s cue is Experimental fixture data, not a RuleConfig baseline value.
- Do not count cue time as EnergyRush collection time.
- Puzzle input must be disabled during the cue.
## Change B — 8.0s Energy Rush Countdown
On interactive EnergyRush entry:
- create the existing fresh Energy-only 6×6 board,
- start a visible countdown at **8.0 seconds**,
- enable Energy puzzle input,
- keep existing Experimental conversion: 1 valid Energy pair → +1 charge for that Energy ID,
- display the countdown prominently near the phase title / Stored Energy section.
When countdown reaches 0:
- disable Energy puzzle input immediately,
- transition automatically to BattleSetup exactly once,
- preserve EnergyQueue charges,
- do not require a Continue button.
The old `Continue to Battle Setup` control may remain in code for historical/debug compatibility but must not appear in the live P1-V1 path.
## Keep Unchanged
- Beast Rush Combo: 5.0s initial / +0.3s per valid match / 5.0s cap.
- BeastQueue behavior.
- Onet + deadlock behavior.
- STAR-001 = 1 / 3 / 9.
- Energy match → +1 charge Experimental Variant A.
- BattleSetup formation logic.
- autonomous Battle fixtures.
- Frontline Heal.
- P1 Session Metrics behavior unless a compatibility fix is required.
## Timer Behavior
- Countdown begins only after the transition cue finishes.
- Timer clamps at 0.0 and must not display negative time.
- If a match resolves near timeout, count it only if the valid match was accepted before the EnergyRush phase locks.
- Transition to BattleSetup must be idempotent: no duplicate transition if timer/update fires again.
- Paused/non-Energy phases must not keep reducing this timer.
## Visible UI
Transition cue:
- centered and readable,
- no board interaction underneath,
- no gameplay button needed.
EnergyRush:
- phase label = `Energy Rush`,
- helper text may say `Collect Energy for Battle`,
- visible timer, e.g. `TIME 8.0`,
- Stored Energy counts stay visible,
- no manual Continue button in the live variant.
Do not polish beyond readability.

9. countdown decreases only during active EnergyRush,

12. EnergyQueue persists into BattleSetup,

14. Restart clears cue/timer state and begins fresh BeastRush,
15. P1-S0 through P1-S5 relevant regressions remain green.
