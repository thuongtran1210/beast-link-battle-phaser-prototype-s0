# 03.3 — Experimental Variant P1-V1 — Pre-Battle Phase Clarity

> **Status:** Proposed Experimental Variant — not adopted into Current Gameplay Spec.
**Source evidence:** <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3ec2674d32c381048288f33c8c133cd8"/>
**Validation Log:** <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3ec2674d32c381da8c14df447e09cb92">03 — Validation Log — Beast Link Battle</mention-page>
**Design Source:** <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3ec2674d32c3812296a8de866c65c434"/>
## Problem
P01 pilot exposed three pre-Battle issues:
1. Beast Rush felt too short.
2. Beast Rush → Energy Rush had no clear phase-change cue.
3. Energy Rush had no readable endpoint and therefore felt open-ended.
Only items 2 and 3 should be changed in the next variant. Beast Rush timing remains unchanged so the next session isolates phase signaling and Energy termination.
## P1-V1 — Pre-Battle Phase Clarity Variant
### Change A — Phase Transition Cue
Before Energy Rush becomes interactive, show a short validation-only transition card:
**ENERGY RUSH**
**Collect Energy for Battle**
Duration fixture: **1.0 second**.
Status: Experimental / UX validation fixture.
### Change B — Explicit Energy Rush Endpoint
Replace the prototype-only manual continuation as the primary end rule with an Experimental fixed collection window:
- Energy Rush becomes active on phase entry.
- Visible countdown = **8.0 seconds**.
- Valid Energy matches still use the current Experimental 1 pair → +1 charge conversion.
- When the timer reaches 0, automatically transition to Battle Setup.
- Show the timer prominently during Energy Rush.
Status: Experimental / prototype-only timing fixture.
### Keep Unchanged
- Beast Rush Combo = 5.0s initial / +0.3s per successful match / 5.0s cap.
- BeastQueue rules.
- STAR-001 1 / 3 / 9.
- Energy match → +1 charge Experimental Variant A.
- BattleSetup formation.
- Autonomous Battle.
- Frontline Heal.
## Why This Variant
- The phase cue targets transition comprehension without changing resource rules.
- A fixed Energy window gives the phase a clear beginning and end.
- Keeping Beast Rush timing unchanged avoids changing two pacing variables at once.
- P02 can then tell us whether Beast Rush still feels too short after the overall pre-Battle structure becomes clearer.
## P02 Validation Focus
- Does the tester notice the Beast → Energy phase transition without moderator help?
- Can the tester explain that Energy Rush is a limited collection window?
- Does the countdown create useful urgency or unwanted stress?
- Does Beast Rush still feel disproportionately short compared with Energy Rush?
- Does the player understand what happens when Energy timer reaches 0?
## Decision Rule
Do not adopt the 1.0s transition cue or 8.0s Energy timer from P01 alone.
After P02/P03 evidence:
- keep / tune / reject the transition cue,
- keep / tune / replace the Energy end rule,
- only then consider a separate Beast Rush timing variant if the short-window issue repeats.
