# BEAST_VISUAL_GRAMMAR.md

## Purpose

Keep player Beast roles readable while allowing each Beast to have unique identity.

Role is not the full character design. Each Beast also needs its own 2–3 motifs.

## Tanker

Shape language:
- round + square
- broad/stable
- lower center of gravity
- wider forearms/shoulders
- grounded stance

Readability motifs:
- guard
- shield
- brace
- sturdy armor/crest

Avoid:
- sharp needle silhouette as the dominant language
- overly thin limbs
- floating pose that removes weight

### Snowguard anchor direction

Current first Master Reference target.

Desired:
- sturdy creature
- cool cream / sky family
- electric-cyan controlled accent
- guard/shield motif
- snow/ice motif used sparingly
- calm determined face
- no excessive ice crystals
- no giant fantasy armor

## Assassin

Shape language:
- triangle
- forward lean
- asymmetric attack direction
- sharp ear/horn/tail or weapon profile
- compressed ready-to-pounce stance

Motifs:
- fang
- slash
- crescent
- shadow edge

Avoid:
- bulky square Tank silhouette
- huge armor mass

### Nightfang anchor direction

Desired:
- dark restrained base
- one readable sharp accent
- shadow/fang/crescent motif
- playful-dangerous expression rather than horror

## Ranger

Shape language:
- compact
- clear horizontal/action direction
- visually lighter than Tanker
- readable projectile/aiming source

Motifs:
- aim
- spark
- projectile
- compact bow/blaster-like language

Avoid:
- giant firearm realism
- military-tech detail density

### Sparkshot anchor direction

Desired:
- bright controlled electrical accent
- compact ranged identity
- clear firing direction
- energetic focused expression

## Mage

Shape language:
- circle + curve
- floating/upward visual rhythm
- open upper silhouette
- controlled trailing shapes

Motifs:
- orb
- star
- bloom
- halo/ring

Avoid:
- particle cloud replacing the silhouette
- excessive robe detail

### Starweaver anchor direction

Desired:
- star/orb motif
- lavender/sky family
- controlled floating element
- curious/composed expression

## Uniqueness rule

Two Beasts with the same Role must not differ only by color.

Use:
- species silhouette
- facial expression
- signature motif
- tail/ear/horn language
- one unique accessory or body feature

while preserving the shared Role grammar.
