# ENEMY_VISUAL_GRAMMAR.md

## Purpose

Enemy art prioritizes tactical readability over mascot personality.

Enemy archetype should be readable before text.

## Shared enemy principles

Compared with player Beasts:
- less personality detail
- more restrained saturation
- simpler motif count
- stronger archetype silhouette
- no dependence on color alone

## Frontliner

Shape:
- square / heavy
- wide mass
- stable
- forward-blocking presence

Motifs:
- shield
- plate
- bulk
- heavy forelimb/body

Read:
“this unit occupies/pressures the frontline.”

## Diver

Shape:
- triangle / sharp
- forward vector
- lean/compressed
- aggressive negative space

Motifs:
- claw
- dagger/fang
- dive arrow feeling
- pounce posture

Read:
“this unit wants to penetrate toward the backline.”

## Ranged

Shape:
- narrow / taller
- clear firing source
- rear-oriented stance
- strong directional weapon/aim line

Motifs:
- crosshair
- bow/projectile
- long-range emitter

Read:
“this unit creates pressure from distance.”

## Enemy family consistency

All three archetypes should feel like the same faction/family through:
- shared outline/shading system
- related neutral palette
- consistent eye treatment
- consistent material simplification

Do not make Frontliner a medieval creature, Diver a sci-fi assassin and Ranged a realistic archer unless a later faction system explicitly requires that.
